== Description

Workloads with predictable baseline utilization MUST be evaluated for Reserved Instances, Reserved VM Instances, Committed Use Discounts, or Savings Plans. The reservation decision and coverage ratio MUST be documented in IaC or a FinOps register. Unused reservations MUST be reviewed quarterly and reallocated or sold where supported. Expiration dates MUST be tracked to avoid lapse into on-demand pricing.

== Rationale

[cols="1,3"]
|===
|Risk |Description

|*Risk* |Persistent on-demand usage for predictable workloads wastes up to 60-70% of compute budget.

|*Risk* |Unmonitored reservation expiration silently reverts workloads to expensive on-demand rates.

|===

== Requirements

* Workloads with predictable baseline utilization MUST be evaluated for Reserved Instances, Reserved VM Instances, Committed Use Discounts, or Savings Plans.
* The reservation decision and coverage ratio MUST be documented in IaC or a FinOps register.
* Unused reservations MUST be reviewed quarterly and reallocated or sold where supported.
* Expiration dates MUST be tracked to avoid lapse into on-demand pricing.

== Implementation Guidance

. Document the requirement in a version-controlled policy, ADR, or standard.
. Assign ownership and establish a review cadence (e.g., quarterly).
. Implement the required configuration as code in Terraform or equivalent IaC.
