== Description

Batch, report generation, HPC, and data processing workloads SHOULD be scheduled during off-peak or high-renewable-energy hours. Scheduler configurations MUST document SLA constraints and acceptable time windows. Preemptible/Spot capacity SHOULD be combined with off-peak scheduling where feasible. Energy and cost savings of time-shifting MUST be tracked and reported.

== Rationale

[cols="1,3"]
|===
|Risk |Description

|*Risk* |Running batch workloads during peak hours wastes energy from high-carbon grid mix and increases cost.

|*Risk* |Missing scheduling constraints can violate SLA or delay critical business processes.

|===

== Requirements

* Batch, report generation, HPC, and data processing workloads SHOULD be scheduled during off-peak or high-renewable-energy hours.
* Scheduler configurations MUST document SLA constraints and acceptable time windows.
* Preemptible/Spot capacity SHOULD be combined with off-peak scheduling where feasible.
* Energy and cost savings of time-shifting MUST be tracked and reported.

== Implementation Guidance

. Document the requirement in a version-controlled policy, ADR, or standard.
. Assign ownership and establish a review cadence (e.g., quarterly).
. Implement the required configuration as code in Terraform or equivalent IaC.
