== Description

Every critical workload MUST have a documented disaster recovery plan with defined RTO and RPO. DR plans MUST be exercised at least annually through tabletop, failover, or full recovery tests. Test results and corrective actions MUST be documented and tracked to completion. DR runbooks MUST be reviewed after each test and after significant architecture changes.

== Rationale

[cols="1,3"]
|===
|Risk |Description

|*Risk* |Untested DR plans often miss critical gaps in network, identity, or data dependencies.

|*Risk* |Outdated DR procedures extend recovery time and increase data loss during real disasters.

|===

== Requirements

* Every critical workload MUST have a documented disaster recovery plan with defined RTO and RPO.
* DR plans MUST be exercised at least annually through tabletop, failover, or full recovery tests.
* Test results and corrective actions MUST be documented and tracked to completion.
* DR runbooks MUST be reviewed after each test and after significant architecture changes.

== Implementation Guidance

. Document the requirement in a version-controlled policy, ADR, or standard.
. Assign ownership and establish a review cadence (e.g., quarterly).
. Implement the required configuration as code in Terraform or equivalent IaC.
