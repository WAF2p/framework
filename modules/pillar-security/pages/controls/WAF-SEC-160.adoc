== Beschreibung

All authentication, authorization, data access, and administrative activity logs MUST be forwarded to a central SIEM or log platform. Log integrity MUST be ensured by write-once storage, cryptographic hashing, or immutable retention. Forwarding MUST be configured so that logs cannot be disabled or altered at the source without triggering an alert. Retention periods MUST meet compliance and forensic requirements.

== Begründung

[cols="1,3"]
|===
|Risiko |Beschreibung

|*Risk* |Locally stored logs can be deleted or modified by attackers to hide evidence of compromise.

|*Risk* |Missing centralized log correlation prevents timely detection of multi-stage attacks.

|===

== Anforderungen

* All authentication, authorization, data access, and administrative activity logs MUST be forwarded to a central SIEM or log platform.
* Log integrity MUST be ensured by write-once storage, cryptographic hashing, or immutable retention.
* Forwarding MUST be configured so that logs cannot be disabled or altered at the source without triggering an alert.
* Retention periods MUST meet compliance and forensic requirements.

== Implementierungsleitfaden

. Document the requirement in a version-controlled policy, ADR, or standard.
. Assign ownership and establish a review cadence (e.g., quarterly).
. Implement the required configuration as code in Terraform or equivalent IaC.
